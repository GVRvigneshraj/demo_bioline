About Us

Trusted Diagnostics Since 2008

Bioline Laboratory is a NABL accredited, automated clinical laboratory serving over 50 Lakh+ families across 150+ branches nationwide.

Our Story

Founded in 2008, Bioline Laboratory started as a small diagnostic center with a vision to make quality healthcare accessible to every household. Over the past 16 years, we have grown into one of the most trusted names in diagnostic testing.

Today, we operate 150+ branches across the country with a team of 500+ certified professionals including pathologists, biochemists, microbiologists, and trained phlebotomists.

Our commitment to accuracy, speed, and affordability has earned us the trust of over 50 Lakh+ families. We process millions of samples annually using fully automated systems that ensure precision at every step.

At Bioline, we believe that quality diagnostics should be a right, not a privilege. That's why we continuously invest in technology, talent, and infrastructure to bring world-class testing to your doorstep.
