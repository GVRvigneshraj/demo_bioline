Help Center

Common questions answered

Everything you need to know about booking, reports, accreditation and our service areas.

How do I schedule a home sample collection?add
Tap "Book a Test", choose your panel, pick a slot, and confirm your address. A certified phlebotomist arrives at your doorstep with sterile, single-use kits — seven days a week, with no prepayment required.
Is Bioline Laboratory NABL accredited?add
Yes. Bioline has been NABL-accredited since 2008 and is ICMR-approved. Every test follows stringent quality-control protocols on fully automated analysers, and each report carries pathologist validation.
When will I receive my report?add
Routine panels are delivered within 24 hours of sample collection. Specialty and advanced tests take 48–72 hours depending on the assay. You'll receive reports by email and WhatsApp, and they're always available in your patient portal.
What is the cancellation and rescheduling policy?add
You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 2 hours before your booked slot. Since no prepayment is required, there are no refund hassles — simply rebook a convenient time.
Do you offer corporate or bulk testing packages?add
Yes. We partner with 300+ hospitals and corporates for wellness camps, pre-employment screening and chronic-disease monitoring. Our B2B team designs custom panels and on-site collection drives tailored to your workforce.
Which cities do you cover?add
We operate 150+ branches across major Indian cities including Chennai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Coimbatore, Madurai, Calicut, Ernakulam, Trichy, Salem and Trivandrum — with home collection available throughout each service zone.
